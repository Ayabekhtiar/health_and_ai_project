What this tool does not do

It does not estimate the benefit of transplantation, and it does not assess fitness for transplant: age, performance status, comorbidity (HCT-CI) and donor availability are not in the model and remain the MDT's judgement. The estimate is a prognosis under the care received in a retrospective 23-centre cohort in which treatments, including transplantation, were not recorded. Threshold 30% reflects the IPSS-M Moderate-Low/Moderate-High boundary at which early transplant was found to prolong life expectancy (Tentori et al., JCO 2024).
